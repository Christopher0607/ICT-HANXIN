逐年Year by year

11 年裡 8 年的淨損益為正。以「每筆平均 R」計是 9 年為正，換停損規則（固定點數、回踩極值）還是 8–9 年 —— 它不是只在某一組參數上成立。8 of eleven years are positive in dollars. Measured as mean R a trade it is nine, and still eight or nine when the stop rule is swapped for a fixed distance or the pullback extreme. It does not live in one parameter set.

你原本要的高勝率The high win rate you asked for

同一批交易，只改停利。勝率單調上升，期望值單調下降 —— 0.25R 的停利確實給你 78.8% 的勝率，代價是每筆 −0.027R。停利拉近讓觸及機率上升的幅度，幾乎剛好等於每筆賺得變少的幅度。The same trades with only the target moved. The win rate rises monotonically and the expectancy falls: a 0.25R target really does hit 78.8% of the time, at −0.027R a trade. Moving the target closer raises the hit rate by very nearly the amount it shrinks the win.

這份東西是什麼、不是什麼What this is and is not

  • 這是搜尋，不是預先註冊。900 組參數在我已經看過的資料上比過，最好的那一格按定義就是最幸運的一格。registry.py 那七個策略是先寫死再跑的，這個不是。A search, not a pre-registration. 900 combinations compared on data already examined; the top cell is by construction the luckiest draw. The seven in registry.py were specified before their results existed. This was not.
  • 2026 年是負的。所有變體都一樣。最近的一年是最弱的一年，而那正是離你下個月開始交易最近的一段。2026 is negative in every variant. The most recent year is the weakest one, and it is the stretch closest to when you would start.
  • 沒有 forward test。LTF Sweep 有 scripts/forward_log.py在跑，從 2026-08-29 之後的每一根新 K 棒都是乾淨的檢驗。這個還沒有。No forward test yet. LTF Sweep has one running in scripts/forward_log.py, where every bar after 2026-08-29 is a clean check. This has nothing equivalent.
  • 成交模型偏向它。停損單進場算了一檔滑價，但真實的停損單在快市裡滑得更多，而這個策略正好進在剛被突破反轉的價位附近。The fill model favours it. Stop entries are charged one tick, and a real stop in a fast market pays more -- which is exactly the condition this model enters in.
