爆了再買，這一年要花多少Busting and re-buying: what the year would have cost

同一批交易照原順序走，每次觸限就買一個新帳戶。在 $900 的單筆風險下是9 個帳戶、106 天，最後一個通過了。再買這套做法行得通 —— 只是帳戶數和月數都比「打亂順序」的估計多，因為虧損的那一季是連在一起來的，不是分散開的。Walking the same trades in order and buying a fresh account after every breach. At $900 a trade that is 9 accounts over 106 days, and the last one passed. Re-buying works — it is just more accounts and more months than a shuffled estimate suggests, because the losing quarter arrives as one run rather than spread out.

這裡是 Standard 便宜 $175。啟用費是「每拿到一個 funded 帳戶收一次」，所以一連串的失敗永遠不會付到它 —— No-Activation 用每個月多 $36、而且每次重置也多 $36，去買掉一個「贏了之後才會欠」的費用。只有當月數加上付費重置次數少於大約四次時它才划算；這一年需要 9 次。Standard is $175 cheaper here. The activation fee is charged once per funded account earned, so a run of failures never pays it — No-Activation spends $36 more every month AND on every reset to buy away a fee you only owe after you have already won. It comes out ahead only while months plus paid resets stay under about four; this year needed 9.

這是一年裡的一條路徑，不是一個分布。它在這裡是為了給出量級 —— 幾百美金、幾個月 —— 不是拿來挑倉位大小的。One path through one year, not a distribution. It is here for the order of magnitude — hundreds of dollars and several months — not to pick a position size from.

這份東西是什麼、不是什麼What this is and is not

  • 這是回測，不是實績。這裡沒有任何一張單真的送出去過。七個 TopstepX 端點都是照文件寫的，從來沒有對過真的伺服器。A backtest, not a track record. No order here was ever sent. The seven TopstepX endpoints are written from documentation and have never run against the live server.
  • 成交模型偏向它。限價單在價格「碰到」時就算成交；真實的限價單要排隊，有時候根本不會成交。The fill model favours it. A limit order fills when price touches it; a real one waits in a queue and sometimes never fills at all.
  • 資料源不同。這是 Databento GLBX.MDP3 的 K 棒，不是 TopstepX 的行情。掃針只要穿透一檔就成立，而那正是兩個資料源最容易不一致的地方。Different data. These are Databento GLBX.MDP3 bars, not TopstepX's feed. A sweep qualifies on one tick of penetration, which is exactly where two feeds disagree.
  • 一致性規則沒有算進這裡。單日獲利不得超過獲利目標的 55%（50K 是 $1,650），超過的話目標會往上調。一天一單、1:1 停利，所以單日獲利約等於單筆風險 —— $900 還有空間，但這條規則在 $1,650/單 附近設了天花板。The consistency target is not modelled here. A single day may not exceed 55% of the profit target ($1,650 on a 50K) or the target rises. One trade a day at a 1:1 target makes a winning day about one unit of risk, so $900 has room — but it caps position size near $1,650 a trade.
  • 資料截止 2026-09-08。最後一季是不完整的。Data ends 2026-09-08. The last quarter is partial.
  • 完整 11 年是 −$3,044，2,161 筆交易、5 年虧損，而且累計曲線從來沒有轉正過。三個月視窗的中位獲利因子是 0.996 —— 等於擲硬幣。當前這個視窗落在那些視窗的第 98 百分位。一個好年頭不能解決這件事；只有還沒有人看過的 K 棒可以。The eleven-year record is −$3,044 across 2,161 trades, with 5 losing years and a cumulative curve that never went positive. The median three-month window has a profit factor of 0.996 — a coin flip. This window sits in the 98th percentile of those. One good year does not settle that; only bars nobody has seen yet can.
