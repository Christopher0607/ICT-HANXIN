# Topstep 自動化政策詢問信

寄給 support@topstep.com（或站上的 Help / Contact 表單）。

**為什麼要問**：我查到的都是二手評測站，2026 年內政策改過幾次，
而且「funded 帳戶」在 Topstep 有 Express Funded 和 Live Funded 兩種，
兩者規則不同。書面答覆是唯一可靠的依據。

**三個關鍵問題**：三個階段分開問、VPS 單獨問、「semi-automated」的定義問清楚。
最後一項最容易含糊帶過 —— 很多回覆會說「semi-automated 可以」，
但不說訊號由程式產生、下單也由程式送出算不算。

## 英文版（直接複製）

**Subject:** Automation policy — Combine, Express Funded, and Live Funded

Hello,

I am preparing to trade a rule-based futures strategy on Topstep and I would
like to confirm your automation policy in writing before I start, so that I do
not build something that turns out to be against the rules.

My setup: a TradingView Pine Script generates the signals, and a small program
of mine receives the alert and places the order through the TopstepX /
ProjectX API. Every order is a limit entry with an attached stop and target.
It trades MNQ, at most one position at a time, and at most one trade per day.
There is no high-frequency component and no latency arbitrage — the signal is
evaluated once per closed 1-minute bar.

Could you please confirm, for each stage separately:

1. **Trading Combine** — may I place orders automatically through the
   TopstepX / ProjectX API?

2. **Express Funded Account** — may I place orders automatically through the
   TopstepX / ProjectX API?

3. **Live Funded Account** — may I place orders automatically through the
   TopstepX / ProjectX API?

And two questions that apply to all three:

4. **Where the program runs.** I have seen it stated that VPS, VPN and remote
   servers are not permitted. Does that apply to all three stages? If a
   program is permitted, does it have to run on my own machine at my own
   location, or may it run on a hosted server?

5. **What counts as "semi-automated".** Some of your material distinguishes
   semi-automated from fully automated trading. In my case the signal is
   generated by a program and the order is submitted by a program, with no
   click from me at the moment of entry, although I monitor it and can stop it
   at any time. Which category does that fall into under your rules?

I would rather find out now than after I have paid for an evaluation, so a
clear yes or no on each point would be very helpful.

Thank you,
[你的名字]
[帳號 email / Topstep 使用者名稱]
